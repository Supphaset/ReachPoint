# Gaps and assumptions

Online research only; no interview, field observation, adoption or operational efficacy evidence has been collected by this team.

| Hypothesis | Current evidence | How to challenge it |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinators struggle to reconcile reports and fulfillment | IFRC guidance supports general feedback/coordination difficulties; household-level correction frequency and local consequences remain unverified | Find incident reports/current SOPs documenting the specific failure and existing fixes; see the [deeper review](relief-ledger-deep-dive.md) |
| A reviewed ledger improves on chat/spreadsheets | Synthetic semantic examples only | Compare error rate and effort on identical scenarios |
| Existing response tools leave this gap | PhonPhai, Sahana, SCOPE and Kobo document significant overlap; exact conversation-reconciliation gap remains unknown | Inspect demos/docs for corrections, partial delivery and confirmed outcomes; compare against structured linked records |
| Two DS teammates can deliver the requested breadth | No approved MVP or feasibility estimate | Bound one workflow; test telephone region/cost constraints before promising it |
| Medical coordination can be demonstrated responsibly | No qualified partner/protocol | Define non-clinical intake/referral boundary and qualified governance; defer unsupported claims |
| Flood context can support decisions | No integration verified | Find licensed sources with measurement reference, coverage, freshness and uncertainty |
| A deployment owner will maintain it | Unknown | Identify operator, procurement/adoption path, maintenance and budget |

Keep research findings separate from inferred opportunities. Awards and marketing descriptions do not prove real use. Absence from a public page does not establish missing functionality.

Potential direction-changing evidence: reconciliation already works adequately; the dominant pain is connectivity/rescue capacity; confirmation adds unacceptable workload; household identity cannot be established safely; no viable owner or inbound-call path exists; the two-person scope cannot satisfy the event requirements honestly.
