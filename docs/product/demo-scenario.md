## Frozen demonstration policies and prerequisites

These rules are synthetic test definitions, not operational standards. At scenario clock 10:35, a relevant assessment is current when its observation is within 60 minutes and its recorded polygon/subset covers the selected assessment scope under the same boundary version. B's observation covers the selected B scope; it does not claim unobserved neighbors are safe. A1 covers only subset A-01; remaining A scope stays unassessed. An unknown boundary/time/policy cannot pass this rule. The extent observation has a separate 120-minute synthetic freshness window; the population reference year remains 2025 and does not become current occupancy.

Before offering M1 v1, record reviewed scenario checks OP-01 at 10:26: H5's L3 reference clarified; T1 released from A1; V1 availability/capacity for the reported three-person assistance scope; scoped movement authorization; D1 acceptance for that scope; no overlapping active commitment. These are fictional, attributed prerequisites, not an inferred safe route or clinical transport authorization. M3 remains a later draft with capacity/time pending. If any required check is unknown, v1 is held rather than presented as ready. V1 failure invalidates its prerequisite; replacement movement/resource checks are unknown until separately reviewed.

## Scenario sequence and expected state

1. Situation selects A: potential exposure, no current relevant assessment. No incoming household requests from A. Coordinator creates assessment task A1 with owner T1, defined scope, resource/access checks and synthetic authorization basis; acknowledgment is version-specific. Unknown access would block offering an actionable field plan; no route is generated.
2. Simulated field update FLD-01 at 10:15: A1 observed one building subset and discovered household H5 at L1. The coordinator reviews individual event proposals and creates a provisional case association. A1’s scoped task outcome is reviewed; A remains **partially assessed**, not safe/complete. The team is released from A1 explicitly before any new commitment.
3. Draft M1 is created for H5, planned pickup L1 and intended destination D1, proposed T1/V1. It remains draft, with no arrival promise or team acknowledgment. H5 initially has assistance requested. The medical-help statement first arrives in CALL-01 and is preserved only after its event review.
4. Real inbound controlled call CALL-01 uses a case reference plus clarification; caller ID is not identity. Original synthetic Thai statement: “ตอนนี้ครอบครัวเราย้ายมาที่อาคารชุมชนท่าเรือแล้ว มีสามคน เพื่อนบ้านยังอยู่ที่เดิม เราใช้โทรศัพท์เครื่องเดียวกัน คนหนึ่งในครอบครัวต้องการความช่วยเหลือทางการแพทย์”. Reviewable translation: “Our family is now at Harbor Community Building; there are three of us. Our neighbor stayed at the old place. We share a phone. A family member needs medical help.”
5. Clarification establishes that “we” is H5 and the neighbor is a different, still-unidentified household. Exact move time remains unknown unless answered. Approved attributed event updates H5’s current reported location L1→L3; M1 planned pickup stays L1 and is flagged for review. Neighbor statement stays an unverified area/proxy report, not an invented case. After review of its own event, medical help is recorded as reported/unassessed, separate from assistance.
6. Coordinator reviews M1 constraints and explicitly revises planned pickup to L3, offers brief v1, and T1 acknowledges v1. M1 remains not started. M3 is still only a later sequential draft.
7. Authenticated T1 report TEAM-01 at 10:30 says V1 unavailable and M1 not started. Coordinator accepts attributed operating update. Exact explicit impact: M1 and M3 require V1; M2 does not. T1’s general availability does not automatically change. Task progress and acknowledgment history remain.
8. No replacement is confirmed. Coordinator records blocked transport/verification owner, creates desk-owned VFY-01 to check a replacement and revises M1 brief to v2 “Hold; replacement resource not confirmed.” T1 acknowledges v2; it does not mean transport started or the household received help. Each reassessment reason needs an explicit review decision; completing VFY-01 alone does not clear all flags.
9. Case drawer: assistance still open; medical help unassessed; qualified referral recipient not confirmed; no confirmed receipt or care. H4 still lacks a confirmed option; Area A remains partially assessed; M2 unchanged.
