# Open product decisions

Current workflow: [volunteer planning and emergency response](volunteer-workflow-proposal.md). The hybrid team-planned routine relief/coordinator-assigned emergency direction is accepted. Open: response-mode evidence/authority, routine commitment review, source-backed depth/needs, capacity assumptions, qualified decisions, resident status window/channel and actual effort within the approved v1 scope.

Prototype v1 inclusion/deferrals and five experiences are settled in [decision 0005](../decisions/0005-prototype-scope-proposal.md). Remaining operational/technical questions below are not settled. Record human-approved conclusions in [decisions](../decisions/README.md), then update the relevant canonical document.

| Priority | Decision | Evidence or outcome needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which coordinator, organization and flood-response situation? | One actor, place, response phase, shift, language/connectivity constraints, recurring costly failure and documented current workaround. Who operates, funds and adopts it? |
| 2 | Is report-to-outcome reconciliation a real underserved problem? | Online evidence of duplicate/conflicting reports or lost fulfillment; compare existing tools and spreadsheet/chat workflow without inventing competitor gaps. What finding would make us abandon/pivot? |
| 3 | Who may establish household identity and authoritative outcomes? | Merge/split rules with shared phones/displacement, evidence, review/correction and receipt responsibilities, dispute handling and audit history. |
| 4 | What can medical and flood context responsibly support? | Qualified review/partner requirements, non-clinical scope, uncertainty visibility, measurement sources/freshness/reference and prohibited claims. Decide what cannot be demonstrated honestly. |
| 5 | Can the approved two-person scope pass its technical gates? | V1 inclusion/deferrals and demo are accepted in decision 0005. Verify real inbound-call feasibility, bounded source access, connectivity recovery, effort and comparisons against manual/simple extraction baselines. No implementation before separate authorization. |

## Follow-up questions

- What happens to failed, cancelled, expired, partial or overlapping commitments, unavailable resources and conflicting units?
- Who confirms receipt versus reported delivery, and what happens if the household cannot be reached?
- How are reported urgency, unassessed conditions and available response options kept distinct?
- What access, location sharing, recording consent, retention and deletion rules fit the selected operator?
- Can telephone calls work in the chosen region and budget? Which failure modes need human handoff?
- What happens after interrupted uploads, delayed sync, retry, duplicate messages and simultaneous edits?
- How do we measure benefit over simpler methods: identity errors, incorrect quantities, premature closure, review time, unresolved uncertainty and missed follow-up?
- Who owns deployment, operating costs, support and sustainability after judging?

- How are areas/people absent from incoming reports identified for assessment, who owns outreach, and what evidence supports scope/recency without claiming universal coverage? See [proposal](../research/unreported-needs.md).
