# Product brief

## Accepted direction

ReachPoint is the working name; Sustainability & Social Impact is the selected hackathon theme. Two Data Scientists will use GitHub and AI development tools. Discovery is online only: no interviews, firsthand response experience or confirmed clinical partner.

## Working problem hypothesis

Organized volunteer team leaders may lack a joined view of affected areas, uncovered needs, existing response coverage and the resources/capabilities needed for a useful daily mission. Residents need to request urgent assistance and understand what response is actually confirmed; coordinators need to validate reports and allocate suitable teams as conditions change. These are the human's requested product jobs; actual pain, adoption and willingness to use remain unvalidated.

Accepted operating direction: team leaders plan ordinary relief trips and coordinators assign emergency missions. Teams should be informed about support-in-place versus evacuation needs; the response-mode assessment/policy remains proposed.

Potential planning user: organized response-team leader. Coordinator: coverage, emergency review and allocation. Resident/proxy: request and own-status. Clinical/rescue professionals have distinct qualified responsibilities; no real partner or protocol is established. Exact organization, geography, languages, connectivity and authority remain unresolved.

## Proposed value to investigate

[Daily planning and emergency workflow](../designs/resource-split-and-live-loop.md): turn dated flood/population/field information into explainable area needs, an editable mission/load/capacity plan, coordinated coverage and truthful resident progress. Flood exposure does not establish evacuation demand; medical urgency is not determined by an unqualified coordinator or model. This is a proposed approach, not evidence of uniqueness or effectiveness.

Earlier report reconciliation, commitment history and separate confirmed outcomes remain supporting invariants; they are not the lead product thesis. The operator/beneficiary split now includes team planning and resident status. Paying customer, deployment owner, partner and sustainability model remain unknown.
