# 0004 — Volunteer planning and resident assistance requested

Date: 2026-10-11 (Asia/Bangkok).
Status: Accepted requested jobs/capabilities and hybrid operating direction; detailed policies, final MVP and implementation unapproved.
Human instruction reference: correction that the earlier designs were not what they intended; volunteers need to know affected areas, where help is needed most, quantities to bring and additional medical teams; residents need emergency requests and help timing; coordinators validate and allocate emergencies; flood/population context should support evacuation-capacity planning.

Follow-up human reference: answered yes to team leaders choosing areas/submitting ordinary plans while coordinators assign emergencies; requested enough information to distinguish support at home from evacuation.

## Decision and rationale

Start product design from the daily volunteer-team planning workflow and emergency request/allocation workflow. Team leaders plan ordinary relief trips; coordinators assign emergency missions, as explicitly confirmed. Add requested daily planning/capacity support, resident assistance status/timing and explicit emergency validation/allocation to requirements. Hazard and population information must lead to useful mission planning rather than standalone map displays.

## Proposed approach and alternatives

[Workflow proposal](../product/volunteer-workflow-proposal.md) recommends organized team leaders proposing routine relief trips and coordinators allocating emergencies. The human accepted this hybrid direction. Exact routine commitment/approval mechanics, response-mode evidence and efficiency policy remain proposed; fully centralized planning was considered as an alternative. No specific ranking, clinical protocol, staffing quantity, operational partner or product layout is accepted.

## Consequences and unknowns

The brief and consolidated PRD now point to the workflow proposal. Earlier wireframes/detail/scenario remain preserved as prior design drafts, not current accepted screens. Flood depth/population exposure do not establish actual evacuation demand; qualified clinical/rescue responsibilities and honest arrival estimates remain necessary. Medical dispatch is requested but no real clinical protocol/partner or autonomous triage is approved. Re-estimate workload after the revised scope; no implementation or operational action is authorized.
