## Design brief

Purpose: help a team leader choose a useful area/intervention, prepare a scoped contribution, and coordinate it with emergency allocation and resident help timing. Primary actions: compare unmet needs/coverage → prepare a mission proposal; coordinator reviews/allocates; resident sees accepted scope and current window.

Layout assumptions remain proposed: desktop map/list plus selected-area panel for planning, focused planner, source/review/options columns for the emergency desk, own-task detail and mobile resident request/status. Calm grayscale drawings emphasize decisions rather than final branding; no fonts/colors/framework are selected. Team leader navigation exposes Area map/My missions; coordinator adds Emergency desk. Planner/detail/roster are contextual surfaces, not extra primary navigation. Permissions are intended requirements, not functioning controls.

Content order: assessed response mode and scope, known target versus modeled exposure, received versus committed aid, suitability/readiness, then the next owned action. Urgency and capability fit remain distinct. Inaccessible or silent areas remain visible. Important source/time, uncertainty and actions must be available without hover or map manipulation; list/keyboard alternatives are necessary future requirements.

## Intended interactions and edge states

- Area/list selection → same area record/scope → source detail or planner. Case/search/map coordinates obey role permission; unlocated reports remain in a list. Layer/tiles failure retains source/list/task work.
- Planner edits → recomputed proposed load/gap with explicit cohort/units/window → coordinator overlap/readiness review → team-specific offer/acceptance. Draft values are not authoritative commitments. Stale record/version conflict requires rereading before approval.
- Emergency report → inspect original/translation/source-linked events → clarify/edit/approve each → inspect qualified requirement and candidates → scoped offer → acceptance. Incomplete validation cannot hide suspected urgency; medical/rescue governance is not inferred from coordinator role or a skill tag.
- Team view → acknowledge current saved brief → report own progress/availability/window → authorized review. A field report or team completion does not confirm household receipt/care. A new brief never inherits old acknowledgment automatically.
- Resident own request → accepted assistance and its current window → update location/situation/non-arrival → reviewed change. Before team acceptance show unknown arrival; delay withdraws old current window and assigns follow-up. No public phone-number lookup, callback/push/SMS, continuous GPS or automatic travel ETA is implemented.

Undrawn required implementation states: loading/empty/source failed, denied record, withdrawn/unassigned offer, duplicate/ambiguous association, concurrent review conflict, no suitable capability, unknown capacity/access, disputed outcome and qualified handover/care. Definitions remain in scope/workflow/requirements. These static drawings do not certify accessibility, mobile reflow, secure access or working interactions. Supporting references later: impeccable spatial/interaction/UX-writing guidance; no skill grants implementation authority.
