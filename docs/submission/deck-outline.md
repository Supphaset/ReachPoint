| # / Title | One message | What is on it | Speaker note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Promise | A flood picture that builds itself from what people say. | `01-flood-board.png`: satellite map with the water layer, household dots, the phone. | Prototype, synthetic people, real water data. |
| 2 · Problem | Messages pile up; the picture never forms. | Hat Yai, Nov 2025: tens of thousands of requests across phone, Facebook, LINE; duplicates and silence ([sources](../research/real-rescue-practice.md)). | Requests are not unique people. No operator interviews yet. |
| 3 · What it does | Victims talk; agents build the map; households are told. | The loop: victim → intake agent → building → households → water level → plans → messages. | Families report needs; volunteers review and post plans, then record mission outcomes. |
| 4 · The judge's minute | Report a case and watch it land. | `04-chat-house.png`: chat with quoted facts; the house on the satellite map; "already known, not counted twice". | Neighbour questions find the exact building on the map. |
| 5 · Physics + people | The simulation is the first guess; reports correct it. | `05-sim-vs-reports.png`: Physics only vs Simulation + reports; "reports say 0.6 m deeper (blocked drains)". | Local-inertial shallow water over the lower U-Tapao basin on FABDEM 75 m terrain; IMERG rain, canals and gauge calibration. See [model builder](../../data/scripts/build_v2_basin_sim.py). Not a hydraulic study. |
| 6 · Trust | Not every report is right. | Doubtful labels, leave-one-out check, ranges for possible duplicates, privacy rule. | Urgent requests are never held back for checks. |
| 7 · Outreach | The agents start the conversation. | Timeline: early warning where the simulated water spreads next, rain and river alerts, morning check, lost contact. | Messages are simulated in the app; SMS/LINE/calls not connected. |
| 8 · Evaluation | Measured on a week of synthetic messages with planted mistakes. | Table from [v2-results](../eval/v2-results.md): 1,517/1,522 = 99.7% Gemini-labelled, with rules filling gaps; depth at households 0.27 m (model alone 0.46 m); 306–309 families vs 299 (lower bound within 3%, upper 3.3%); 4/5 wrong reports caught, 5% false; 1% wrong merges; 72/72 neighbour buildings. | The truth is the simulation plus local effects: shows how physics and reports combine, not field accuracy. |
| 9 · Architecture | Google Cloud end to end. | Cloud Run (web + API, always-on, session affinity), Gemini Flash-Lite reader, Flash copilot with function calling, Gemini Live voice, Firestore room persistence, Firebase Hosting CDN, Earth Engine, Maps, Secret Manager. | One container/instance; active rooms in memory, Firestore write-behind. Gemini API key route by default; Vertex optional. Voice socket goes directly to Cloud Run. |
| 10 · Impact & next | Start with one district drill. | Who runs it (foundation or provincial disaster office), cost per 1,000 messages, next steps: responder drill, SMS/LINE channels, stronger authenticated privacy and multi-instance storage. | No adoption or measured time savings yet. |
